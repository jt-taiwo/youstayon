### Additional recommendation

For the detailed ERDs, I recommend using **Mermaid `erDiagram` syntax** throughout the documentation, alongside simplified ASCII diagrams where helpful. Mermaid diagrams are text-based, version-control friendly, render well in Markdown viewers (including GitHub), and can later be imported into documentation tools or converted into graphical diagrams without maintaining separate binary diagram files. This aligns well with our Git-first, documentation-as-code approach.
